Findings

high $412,000 sitting in stalled deals

4 of 20 open deals (20%) have been in their current stage longer than that stage's own historical p75 dwell — the threshold is calibrated from this pipeline's history, not an industry guess.

Quantified: $412,000 — sum of open amounts on the deals in evidence (stall.stalledDeals).

high Largest funnel leak: demo → negotiation converts at 30%

Of 12 deals that entered demo in the analysis cohort, only 30% ever reached negotiation. Cohort discipline: only deals old enough to have resolved (> 270 days) or already closed are counted, so this is not an artifact of young deals still in flight.

high Only 45% of open deals show logged activity in 30 days

14-day coverage is 30%. Measured from the CRM's own last-activity roll-up, so it reflects LOGGED activity: some of this gap is execution, some is logging hygiene — the report treats those as separate problems with separate fixes.

medium demo is the slow stage: p75 dwell 40 days

A quarter of deals spend more than 40 days in demo (median 10d, n=12) — 3.6× this pipeline's own typical stage p75 of 11d.

medium 20% of closed deals moved backwards through stages

Backwards stage moves usually mean stage definitions are being used as a to-do list rather than a state machine, or that qualification happens after commit stages. Cycle times above use total dwell including re-entries (cycle_calc=sum_dwell), so regression does not hide time.

medium 70% of open deals are single-threaded

Measured from contact roles/associations on 18 deals. Single-threaded deals carry champion-departure risk and stall harder in procurement.

medium 40-point win-rate spread across reps

Across 3 reps with at least 5 closed deals each, win rates range from 10% to 50%. A spread this wide usually means the playbook lives in the top performers' heads rather than in the process. Cells under n=5 are suppressed, not shown as noise.

info 14 historical stage events were outside the approved stage map

Stages seen only in history (renamed or retired): "Old Stage" (14). These events are excluded rather than guessed — extend the stage map and re-capture if they should count.

Methodology

All metrics computed from salesforce's own historical records at capture time and frozen; the stored snapshot is immutable at the database level. Cycle times: dwell per stage over closed deals (mode: sum_dwell), re-entries included; regression reported separately. Conversion: cohort-based over deals closed or older than 270 days. Stall: open-deal dwell beyond that stage's own historical p75. Stalled-pipeline dollars: sum of open amounts on stalled deals. Segment cells under n=5 suppressed. Findings thresholds are fixed editorial constants stated in the engine (v: metric defs 1.0.0).
