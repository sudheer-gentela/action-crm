Running Campaigns

Your step-by-step playbook for running prospecting campaigns — create, build the cadence, add prospects, launch, call, and work the replies. Covers AI and manual, auto-send and manual, and signal and plain. Setup lives in the Campaign Setup guide.

01 Before you start — what must be enabled for you

You don't configure these yourself, but your day-to-day work depends on them. Run down this checklist; if something's missing, the last column tells you what to ask an admin for. Full admin details live in the Campaign Setup guide.

Three things you do control that trip people up: (1) your mailbox — you connect and reconnect it; (2) the sending window — a per-campaign schedule (§05) overrides the org default; (3) whether a step auto-sends or waits for your approval — a sequence setting you choose, not an admin one. Also: prospects need a LinkedIn URL saved or the extension can't match them (§04).

02 Install & connect the Chrome extension

Skip this section if you only do email/call outreach. The extension is the bridge between LinkedIn and your pipeline.

  1. Unzip the build so you have the folder containing manifest.json.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, toggle on Developer mode (top-right), click Load unpacked, and select that folder.
  3. The GoWarm flame appears in your toolbar — pin it (puzzle-piece → pin).
  4. Connect: open your GoWarmCRM app in a tab and sign in. Click the extension icon; the popup should read Connected. If it says Not connected, sign in and hit Try connecting now.
ℹ️

Works in Chrome, Edge, or Brave. No token to paste — it uses your session. When a new build ships, the popup shows a banner: replace the folder and click reload on the card at chrome://extensions; your connection and defaults carry over.

03 Create a campaign

Open Prospecting → Campaigns → New Campaign. Two paths — pick by what you're doing.

Guided Brief wizardStaged flow
A straightforward outreach push; capture your pitch fast for AI-quality framing.You want to target by signals, start from a Target Profile, or run a non-outreach motion.

Guided Brief wizard (six steps)

  1. Basics — name (required), the solution it prospects for, an internal description.
  2. Audience & ICP — one or more personas.
  3. Pitch — the core pitch paragraph + value props.
  4. Proof — case studies (customer, problem, what you did, outcome).
  5. Outreach fallback — the plain template baseline; any AI draft reverts to this in one click.
  6. Review — confirm and create.

Staged flow (three steps — signal or plain)

  1. Purpose & basics — name, purpose (outreach vs. non-outreach), solution, description.
  2. Targeting — leave empty for a plain list-driven campaign (you add prospects yourself), or add Filter (must-match) and Prioritize (rank-higher) rules from the signal catalog. Signal campaigns need enrichment enabled by an admin; qualifying prospects surface in your Research Queue to approve.
  3. Execution (outreach only) — attach a playbook, sequence, goal, and schedule.

04 Build the sequence

Prospecting → Sequences → New Sequence. A sequence is an ordered list of steps, each with a channel and a delay.

Steps & channels

✉️
Email — subject + body (merge variables supported).
🔗
LinkedIn — a connection request (optional note) or a DM.
📞
Call — a task for you; no body. Surfaces in your Calls tab / Work Queue (§06).

With AI vs. without AI

📝
Without AI — you write each step; personalization is merge variables. Deterministic. Needs nothing from an admin.
✨
With AI (needs a provider + skill set up) — flip the sequence AI toggle on, set steps to AI mode. The skill drafts from the prospect's context + your pitch. Revert any AI step to its template in one click.

With auto-send vs. without auto-send — your choice, per step

Email. Turn on require approval to review first: the step becomes a draft in your Work Queue to edit and send. Leave approval off to auto-send on schedule (needs a healthy mailbox).

LinkedIn. Manual — the request/DM lands as a task you send yourself; the extension logs it. Auto-send — needs the org enable and your opt-in at Settings → LinkedIn Auto-Connect (with a personal cap); the extension then sends with a hard 24h cap, jittered pacing, working-hours gating, and an instant stop on any LinkedIn security prompt.

Call — always manual: dial via Twilio or log it (§06).

Stop conditions & A/B

💬
Stop on reply (default on) — an email or synced LinkedIn reply stops the enrollment. Leave it on.
🤝
Stop on connection accept — for LinkedIn-led sequences, pause once they accept so you follow up personally.
🧪
A/B variants — test arms via the A/B panel; assignment is deterministic and results roll up per arm.

05 Add prospects & set the schedule

A campaign does nothing until prospects are in it. Mix these freely.

✍️
Manual — open the campaign → Add Prospect. Enter name, company, email, LinkedIn URL, phone.
📄
CSV import — Import (top of Prospecting), map columns. Update-by-ID re-import avoids duplicates.
🧩
Capture from LinkedIn — the panel captures profiles (/in/) and companies (/company/). Set Push defaults in the popup so captures land in the right campaign/sequence.
📡
Research Queue — for signal campaigns, qualifying prospects appear with the triggering signal; review and approve.
⚠️

Every prospect needs a LinkedIn URL. The extension matches on the linkedin_url stored on the record (https://www.linkedin.com/in/username). No URL → the panel shows "Not in GoWarmCRM" even though they exist. Set it on the prospect record. This is the #1 reason the panel "doesn't work."

Set the sending schedule (optional override)

Every campaign already sends on a schedule: its own if set, otherwise your org's, otherwise a built-in default — so a new campaign sends correctly on day one. To use a different window for this campaign, set it in the staged flow's Execution step or the campaign's schedule settings: sending days, timezone, start time, pacing (fixed interval or spread), and the LinkedIn release cap.

ℹ️

The email daily cap isn't here — it lives on each of your sender accounts, and the campaign's capacity is the sum of your active senders' limits. Use Schedule preview to see when steps will actually fire.

06 Launch & run

Enroll prospects, then the system fires due steps on schedule.

1️⃣
One prospect — open them → Enroll into the sequence (pick sequence + start step).
👥
Everyone — Enroll all on the campaign enrolls every eligible prospect.
🌊
In waves — Batch Activate rolls a large set live in controlled waves — good for warming a mailbox.

After launch: auto-send steps go out (within schedule + caps); approval-required and Call steps become tasks in your Work Queue. The Pacing tile and Schedule preview show when upcoming steps fire. Campaigns are Active / Paused / Completed — pause to stop new steps without losing enrollments.

07 Making & logging calls (Twilio)

Requires calling set up by an admin (org subaccount + your number + calling enabled). If the Call via Twilio button isn't there, that's what's missing.

One-time: your phone & timezone

In Settings → My Preferences, set your timezone (so call tasks and callbacks are local) and — only in phone-bridge mode — a personal phone. In the default browser-softphone mode you just need a working microphone.

Place a call

  1. Open the prospect (or a call task).
  2. In the phone panel, pick which number to dial — you can add, label, set-primary, or delete a prospect's numbers here.
  3. Click Call via Twilio. Browser softphone: allow microphone access the first time; it dials the prospect with your number as caller ID. Phone bridge: Twilio rings your phone first, then bridges the prospect.
  4. If recording with disclosure is enabled, a spoken announcement plays at the start.

Log the outcome

Record it in the Log call panel: outcome (connected — meaningful, connected — brief, callback requested, voicemail left, no answer, wrong number, gatekeeper), notes, and — for a callback — a date/time that defaults to the next business day at 10am. Logged calls land on the prospect's timeline and feed your call metrics.

08 Monitor & respond (your daily loop)

Read the health, work the queues, and keep LinkedIn synced.

Health pill on the Campaigns list

Failing
Steps failed in the last 24h — usually a disconnected sender. Reconnect your mailbox yourself in Settings → sender accounts.
Stalled
Active enrollments idle ~7 days — usually drafts/call tasks in your Work Queue or a schedule gap.
Active
Healthy.

Work the queues

📥
Inbox — replies land here; work them (they're on the prospect's timeline).
⚡
Work Queue — drafts/tasks awaiting you (approval-required email, manual LinkedIn, calls).
📞
Calls — call tasks and logging (§07).
📈
Insights / WBR — funnel and weekly-review metrics.

Sync LinkedIn every session — order matters

  1. Open My Network → Manage → Sent on LinkedIn, click Check & update sent. For acceptances, open My Network → Connections, then Check & update accepted. (Each reads the page you have open.)
  2. Resolve identities (runs in batches — re-click to continue), then Sync messages (each message recorded once; safe to re-run).
ℹ️

Sync before you plan the day. Replies only stop sequences once GoWarmCRM has seen them — a morning sync means nobody gets step 3 after they already answered step 2.

10 Troubleshooting

🧩
Extension panel doesn't appear — be on a linkedin.com/in/ URL, refresh, and confirm the extension is enabled at chrome://extensions.
🔍
"Not in GoWarmCRM" — the prospect has no saved LinkedIn URL, or the format doesn't match. Add/fix it (§05).
🔑
"Sign in first" — open the app, sign in, reopen the popup.
🔁
Sync buttons seem to do nothing — identity resolution runs in batches; re-click Resolve identities, then Sync messages.
📭
Nothing is sending — check your mailbox is Healthy (reconnect it yourself if not), you're inside the sending window, and under your sender's daily cap.
😴
Campaign is "Stalled" — work the drafts/call tasks in your Work Queue, or the schedule has no open hours right now.
💬
Replies aren't stopping sequences — for LinkedIn, Sync messages first; for email, confirm reply-stop isn't off for that campaign.
✨
AI drafts look generic — fill in the campaign's pitch, value props, personas, case studies, and hook ordering. (If AI won't draft at all, ask your admin to confirm a provider is configured.)
🔗
LinkedIn auto-send isn't running — org enable + your opt-in both on, within human-hours, under the cap, no security challenge open, LinkedIn open in a normal tab.
📞
No "Call via Twilio" button — ask your admin to provision the subaccount, give you a number, and enable calling for you.
🎧
Call won't connect (softphone) — allow microphone access for the site in the address bar. A network that blocks Twilio (wss://*.twilio.com) can also stop it. In bridge mode, set your personal phone in My Preferences.
