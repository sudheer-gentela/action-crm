Chrome Extension Guide

The GoWarmCRM extension is the bridge between LinkedIn and your pipeline — capture profiles and companies, log every touch, keep connections and messages in sync, and (where your org allows it) let connection requests send themselves, safely.

01 What the extension does

Everything the extension does keeps you on LinkedIn while GoWarmCRM stays current behind the scenes. No copy-pasting profiles, no manually logging "sent a request", no wondering whether someone replied.

👤
Capture — turn the profile or company page you're looking at into a prospect (or a batch of them) in your pipeline, in a couple of clicks.
📌
Log — a floating panel on every profile shows the person's status and timeline, with one-tap logging for requests, connections, messages, and replies.
🔄
Sync — one click reconciles your sent requests, acceptances, and LinkedIn conversations with GoWarmCRM, so sequences react to what actually happened.
🤖
Automate — opt-in connection-request auto-send with hard caps, randomized pacing, working-hours gating, and an instant stop on any LinkedIn security prompt.
ℹ️

The extension works on linkedin.com profile and company pages and talks only to GoWarmCRM. Your session stays in your browser — it reads the pages you visit and logs to your workspace, nowhere else.

02 Install & connect

Installation takes about a minute, and connecting takes zero — the extension picks up your GoWarmCRM session automatically. No token to copy, nothing to paste.

Install

  1. Get the extension folder from your install page or admin, and unzip it if needed — you want the folder containing manifest.json.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode (top-right toggle), click Load unpacked, and select the folder.
  3. The GoWarmCRM flame appears in your toolbar. Pin it (puzzle-piece icon → pin) so it's always one click away.

Connect

  1. Open app.gowarmcrm.com in any Chrome tab and sign in.
  2. The extension detects your session automatically. Click the toolbar icon — the popup should show Connected with your account.
  3. If it says Not connected, sign in to GoWarmCRM, then reopen the popup (there's a Try connecting now button if it needs a nudge).
ℹ️

Works in any Chromium browser — Chrome, Edge, Brave. There's no server setup on your side; the extension talks to the live GoWarmCRM API.

03 The toolbar popup

The popup is the extension's control room — your connection status, the sync actions, and the defaults that decide where captures land.

GoWarmCRM · extension popup
The popup at a glance — status, sync actions, and your capture defaults.
  1. Status — who you're signed in as, and the workspace captures are saved to. There's a quick Open GoWarmCRM link, and the popup will tell you when a new extension version is available.
  2. Connection requests — the Check & update buttons reconcile sent and accepted requests with GoWarmCRM (details below).
  3. LinkedIn messages — Resolve identities and Sync messages harvest your conversations with tracked prospects (details below).
  4. Push defaults — the campaign and sequence new captures land in, plus account and workspace details under Settings.

04 Capture a profile

On any linkedin.com/in/… page, the GoWarm panel appears on the right. If the person isn't in your CRM yet, capturing them takes seconds.

  1. Open the profile. The panel loads and checks whether the person is already a prospect (matched by their LinkedIn URL).
  2. Hit Capture — the extension reads the profile (name, title, company, location, and more) and shows you the fields to review. Edit anything inline before saving.
  3. Save. The prospect lands in your research queue, added to your default campaign and enrolled in your default sequence — or standalone, if you've left the defaults blank.
ℹ️

Already-captured profiles can be refreshed to pull updates — useful when someone changes roles. The panel shows their current status and full outreach timeline either way.

💡

Capture while you browse. The best time to capture is the moment you're already reading someone's profile and know why they're interesting — add a note with the why-now while it's fresh.

05 Capture a company page

Company pages (linkedin.com/company/…) can be captured too — turning a company you're researching into an account with people and signals attached, instead of one prospect at a time.

  1. Open the company page — the capture control appears just like on profiles.
  2. Capture the company — firmographics flow into GoWarmCRM as an account, and employees visible on the page can come along as prospect candidates.
  3. What you capture also feeds the signal store — company activity you observed becomes a why-now your campaigns can gate or rank on.
ℹ️

Company captures are one of the sources behind signal-driven campaigns and the Work Queue — see Signals & the Work Queue in the End-User Guide.

06 The on-page panel

For prospects already in your CRM, the panel is a live timeline and a logging pad — you never leave the profile to keep GoWarmCRM accurate.

linkedin.com/in/ana-ng
The panel on a tracked prospect. Status, timeline, one-tap logging.
  1. Status & timeline — the person's current LinkedIn status and every touch so far, straight from GoWarmCRM.
  2. One-tap logging — record the funnel as it happens: Request sent → Connected → Message sent → Reply received. Each tap updates GoWarmCRM instantly.
  3. Notes — paste the message you sent or the gist of their reply, so the context lives on the prospect, not in your memory.
ℹ️

Matching is by the LinkedIn URL on the prospect record. If the panel says "Not in GoWarmCRM" for someone you know is there, their record is probably missing the URL — add it in the prospect's detail panel.

07 Connection sync

You don't have to log every request by hand. The popup's Check & update buttons read your own sent-invitations and connections lists and reconcile them with GoWarmCRM in bulk.

  1. Check & update sent — marks prospects whose connection requests you've sent, so the funnel reflects reality even for requests sent outside GoWarmCRM.
  2. Check & update accepted — detects acceptances and updates each prospect's status. If a sequence is set to stop on connection accept, this is what triggers it.
💡

End your LinkedIn session with a sync. Two clicks, and every acceptance you collected that day flows into the funnel, your reporting, and your sequences' stop rules.

08 Message sync & replies

LinkedIn replies matter as much as email replies — they should stop sequences and show up in your prospect timelines. Message sync makes that happen.

  1. Resolve identities first — this maps your LinkedIn conversation threads to the prospects GoWarmCRM tracks. It runs in batches (accepted prospects go first); re-click to continue through your list.
  2. Sync messages — harvests the conversations with those prospects into GoWarmCRM's message ledger. Each message is recorded exactly once, so re-running is always safe.
  3. Replies detected here behave like email replies: the prospect's sequence stops (unless the campaign opted out), and the conversation is on their timeline for whoever picks it up.
ℹ️

Only conversations with prospects you're tracking in GoWarmCRM are synced — the rest of your inbox is never read into the system. Connection-request notes are recorded but not counted as messages, so message and reply counts stay honest.

💡

Sync before you plan the day. Replies only stop sequences once GoWarmCRM has seen them — a morning sync means nobody gets step 3 after they already answered step 2.

09 Connection auto-send

When your org enables it and you opt in, the extension can send your queued connection requests for you — paced and gated to look, and behave, like a human.

How it stays safe

🎚️
Double opt-in — your org admin turns on the capability and sets the guardrails; you opt in yourself in Settings → LinkedIn Auto-Connect, where you can also set a personal daily cap. Nothing sends unless both are on.
🧮
Hard daily cap — a rolling-24-hour ceiling per rep. When it's reached, sending stops until headroom returns.
🎲
Randomized pacing — a jittered delay between every send, so there's no mechanical rhythm.
🕗
Human hours — sends only fire inside the working days and hours your admin configured.
🛑
Challenge abort — if LinkedIn shows any security prompt, auto-send halts immediately. No retries; a human resolves it first.
⚠️

Understand the risk. Automating actions on LinkedIn is against LinkedIn's terms of service and carries account risk regardless of guardrails. Keep your cap conservative, keep LinkedIn open in a normal tab while it runs, and treat a security challenge as a signal to slow down.

10 Push defaults

Defaults decide where captures land, so a capture is two clicks instead of five. Set them once in the popup's Settings.

  1. Default campaign — new captures are added here automatically.
  2. Default sequence — captures are enrolled here on activation.
  3. Leave both blank to save captures as standalone prospects you'll organise later.
💡

Match your defaults to your current motion. Running a CRO campaign this month? Point the defaults at it, and every profile you capture during research is already in the right place.

11 Updating

The popup tells you when a new version is available — a banner appears with a link to the install page.

  1. Download the new build from the install page and replace your extension folder with it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and click the reload icon on the GoWarmCRM card (or use the popup's Reload extension button).
  3. Reopen the popup — the version in the footer should now match the latest.
ℹ️

Updating never touches your connection or defaults — they carry over.

12 Troubleshooting

Most issues come down to one of four things — the page, the match, the session, or the version.

SymptomFix
Panel doesn't appearMake sure you're on a linkedin.com/in/… profile (not search or the feed), refresh the page, and check the extension is enabled at chrome://extensions.
"Not in GoWarmCRM" for someone who isThe prospect record is missing its LinkedIn URL, or the URL doesn't match (watch for trailing slashes). Add or fix it on the prospect, then refresh.
"Sign in to GoWarmCRM first"Open app.gowarmcrm.com in a tab, sign in, then reopen the popup. Use Try connecting now if needed.
Logged an event, panel didn't updateIt re-renders after about a second — give it a moment.
Sync buttons seem to do nothingIdentity resolution runs in batches — re-click Resolve identities to continue through your list, then run Sync messages.
Behaving oddly after an updateReload the extension at chrome://extensions and hard-refresh the LinkedIn tab.
ℹ️

Need to dig deeper? The build ships with debug hooks. On any profile page, open DevTools (F12) → Console and run await __gowarmCaptureDebug() — copy the output and share it with support.
